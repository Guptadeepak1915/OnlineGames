How to Play 2048 Merge

2048 Merge is played on a 4×4 grid. Your objective is to slide numbered tiles across the board and merge tiles with identical numbers. When two tiles with the same number touch, they combine into one tile with double the value (e.g. 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8, 8 + 8 = 16, up to 1024 + 1024 = 2048).

After every move, a new tile (either a 2 or a 4) appears in an empty spot on the board. The game is won when you create a tile with the value 2048. You can continue playing beyond 2048 to reach 4096 and higher!

Game Controls

  • On Mobile / Touchscreens: Swipe Up, Down, Left, or Right across the board.
  • On Desktop / Keyboard: Use the Arrow Keys or W, A, S, D.
  • Undo Feature: Made an accidental swipe? Click the Undo button to reverse your last move (up to 3 undos per game).

Winning Strategy: The Corner Anchor Protocol

The most effective strategy to reach 2048 consistently is to designate a permanent corner (such as the bottom-left corner) for your highest value tile:

  • Keep your highest tile pinned to that corner and never move it away.
  • Build a descending snake chain of tiles across that row (e.g., 512 → 256 → 128 → 64).
  • Stick strictly to three directions (e.g., Down, Left, and Up) and avoid pressing the opposite direction (Right) so low-value tiles never spawn behind your mega tile.

Scoring and Local Saves

Whenever two tiles merge, the value of the resulting tile is added directly to your score. Your personal best score is saved locally on your device via your web browser's localStorage, so your record is waiting whenever you return.
