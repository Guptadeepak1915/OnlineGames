Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At GameHub (accessible at https://onlinefreegame.netlify.app/), protecting your privacy is our core operational priority. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is handled, and your rights concerning your personal data when playing our free browser games.

1. Privacy-First Philosophy: No Accounts Required

We believe online gaming should be fun, fast, and frictionless without invasive surveillance. You do not need to register, create an account, or share your name, telephone number, physical address, or password to play any game on GameHub.

2. Data Saved Locally on Your Device (Browser LocalStorage)

Our games save your high scores, personal best records, level unlocks, and gameplay preferences (such as audio mute status and dark mode settings) directly on your device via standard browser localStorage.

  • This data stays strictly on your browser and is never uploaded, transferred, or stored on our external web servers.
  • You retain complete control: clearing your web browser's cache or website data immediately removes all saved scores and settings.

3. Google AdSense, Cookies & Advertising Partners

We fund the free hosting and development of GameHub by displaying advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to serve contextual and personalized ads based on your visit to this site and other websites across the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to GameHub and other websites.
  • You can customize your personalized ad preferences or opt out entirely by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • To learn how Google manages and processes data collected through partner platforms, visit How Google uses information from sites that use our services.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds Choices or Your Online Choices (EU).

4. Server Log Files

Like virtually all standard web hosting platforms, our hosting provider (Netlify) may automatically log standard non-personally identifiable connection metadata. These server logs may include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser application type, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, date/time timestamps, and pages requested. This metadata is utilized solely for system security, detecting malicious traffic, and analyzing overall aggregate performance.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you are entitled to certain privacy rights under regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know / Access: You can request information regarding what data is collected and processed.
  • Right to Deletion: Because GameHub does not collect or store personal profiles on a database, all your user gameplay data is stored in your own device's localStorage, which you can delete immediately from your browser settings.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing: We do not sell your personal data. You can manage or disable cookie tracking via your browser privacy settings or the ad choice links provided above.

6. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

GameHub offers family-friendly puzzle, math, and casual arcade games suitable for general audiences. We do not intentionally target, collect, or solicit personal identification from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes personal information was inadvertently provided, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

7. Security and External Links

Our website is served over secure, encrypted HTTPS protocols. While we strive to protect your security, our website or blog guides may link to third-party resources. We have no authority over and accept no liability for the content, privacy practices, or policies of third-party websites.

8. Updates to this Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changing legal requirements or web technology enhancements. Any revisions will be reflected with the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
