How to play Sudoku

Fill the 9×9 grid so each row, each column, and each 3×3 box contains the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once. Every puzzle has a unique solution. The game accepts only moves forced by the current clues, so guessing is not allowed. Look for a number that is the only candidate in a square or the only place for that number in a row, column, or box.

Levels, lives and score

Play levels begin with easy puzzles and progress through medium to hard. You have 3 lives, and an incorrect move costs a life. Solving a puzzle earns points, with speed and no-mistake bonuses. Hints cost points. Every few levels you earn a life or hint. Practice mode lets you choose a difficulty and provides unlimited hints.

Helpers

When you select a square, its row, column, and 3×3 box are highlighted. Matching numbers are marked, and red indicates a duplicate in a Sudoku unit. Notes help track candidates and are removed from peers when you place a number. Undo restores your previous move, Erase clears a square, and Hint fills the next logically forced square. The number under each button shows how many of that digit remain.

Tips

Start with a row, column, or box that has the most clues. Check which digits are missing and eliminate candidates using all three Sudoku units. A digit that has only one possible square in any row, column, or box is a safe move.
