How to Play Fruit Slice

Step into the dojo and test your reflexes! Juicy tropical fruits are tossed into the air. Swipe across your screen to draw a razor-sharp katana blade trail and slice them in mid-air.

🍉

1. Slice Juicy Fruits

Every sliced fruit awards 1 point. Watermelons, oranges, pineapples, coconuts, and strawberries all splatter luscious juice!

💣

2. Beware the Bombs

Black iron explosive bombs are mixed in with the fruits. Slicing a bomb results in an instant detonation and strike!

⚡

3. Chain Combos

Slice 3 or more fruits in a single swipe to trigger massive Combo bonus points! Watch out for rare Critical +10 slashes.

📤

4. Share & Challenge

Once your game ends, click Share My Score to challenge your friends on WhatsApp, Twitter, or copy your brag card!
