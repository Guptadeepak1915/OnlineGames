About GameHub

GameHub is a small collection of free browser games. Every game opens instantly on a phone or computer. There is no sign up and no download.

The games are built by an independent developer from India. New games are added over time, and each one is made to be quick to learn and hard to put down.

What we care about

  • Games that load fast and work on small phones.
  • No sign up. Your best scores are saved on your own device.
  • Fair ads placed outside the game area, so they never block your play.

If you have feedback, found a bug, or want to suggest a game, please write to us on the Contact page.
