How to play Word Fill

A word appears with some letters missing, for example L _ _ H T. Below the word you see a set of letters. Some of them are the missing letters and some are wrong. Tap the right letters to fill the blanks. When all blanks are full, the game checks your answer. A correct answer gives points. A wrong answer, or running out of time, costs one of your 3 lives. When you have no lives left, the game ends.

Time and score

Pick how much time you get for each word: 15 seconds on Easy, 10 seconds on Medium or 5 seconds on Hard. When a word has more blanks you get 1 extra second for each extra blank. You earn points for every missing letter, plus bonus points for the seconds you have left and for your streak of correct words. Medium multiplies your points by 1.5 and Hard by 2.

Categories

Choose one category to practise, or play Mixed to get words from every category. The categories are Cities, Science, Technology, History, Geography, Animals, Food, Sports and India. The game gets harder as you solve more words, because more letters go missing, up to four blanks in one word.

Tips

Look at the letters you can already see and think about the category. Use the wrong letters to your advantage: if a letter would not fit anywhere, it is probably a decoy. You have 2 hints in every game, so save them for the hardest words. Playing in a category you know well is a great way to learn new spellings too.
