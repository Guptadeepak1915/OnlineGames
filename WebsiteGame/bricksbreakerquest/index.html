    How to play Bricks Breaker Quest

    Move the paddle left and right at the bottom of the screen to keep the ball in play. The ball bounces off the walls, the paddle and the bricks. Every time the ball hits a brick, the brick breaks. Break all the breakable bricks to finish the level. If the ball falls below the paddle you lose a life. You start with 3 lives, and the game ends when you have none left.

    Brick types

    Coloured bricks break with one hit. Blue-grey bricks are tough and need two hits. Orange bricks marked with an X explode and break the bricks next to them. Gold bricks marked with a plus always drop a power-up. Grey steel bricks cannot be broken, so bounce the ball around them.

    Power-ups

    Some broken bricks drop a capsule. Catch it with your paddle to use it. W makes the paddle wider. M splits the ball into more balls. S slows the ball down. F turns the ball into a fireball that passes through bricks. The heart gives you an extra life.

    Quests and stars

    Each level shows a quest card before you start. Clear all bricks to earn one star. Finish without losing a life for a second star, and finish before the target time for a third star. Extra stars give bonus points. Hitting several bricks in a row without touching the paddle builds a combo that multiplies your points up to x5.

    Tips

    Hit the ball with the edge of the paddle to change its angle. Aim for the gold bricks first to get more power-ups. Use explosive bricks to clear large groups. Do not chase every capsule if it puts the ball at risk.
