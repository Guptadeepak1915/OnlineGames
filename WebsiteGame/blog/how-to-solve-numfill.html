How to solve NumFill puzzles step by step

Published October 2026 · 5 min read

NumFill is a number grid puzzle. Some boxes are empty, and you fill them with numbers from 1 to 9. Each row and each column has a clue. This guide shows a method that works on every puzzle.

Step 1: Read the clue correctly

A clue like sum ÷ 3 = 5 means: if you add all numbers in that line and divide by 3, you get 5. So the total of the line is 3 × 5 = 15. A clue like = 11 shows the total directly. Always convert the clue to a total first.

Step 2: Start with lines that have one blank

If a line has only one empty box, the answer is easy. Subtract the visible numbers from the total.

Row: 4, _, 2     clue: sum ÷ 3 = 5
Total = 3 × 5 = 15
Blank = 15 - 4 - 2 = 9

Step 3: Use the new number in the crossing line

Every box belongs to one row and one column. When you solve a box using the row, use that number in the column too. This often opens the next blank.

Column: 9, 7, _   clue: sum ÷ 7 = 3
Total = 7 × 3 = 21
Blank = 21 - 9 - 7 = 5

Step 4: Use the limits of 1 to 9

When a line has two blanks, the limits help. Every number is between 1 and 9. Suppose two blanks must add up to 17. The only pair is 8 and 9, because 9 is the biggest number allowed. If two blanks must add up to 3, the pair is 1 and 2. Small and large totals give you strong hints.

Step 5: Try the other direction

If a row does not help, look at the column that crosses the same blank. One of the two lines often has fewer blanks and gives you the answer.

Step 6: Check green clues

When a line is full and correct, its clue turns green. A red clue means the line is full but the total is wrong. Check the numbers in that line and the lines that cross it.

Lives and hints

You have 3 lives. You lose one when the grid is full and something is wrong. So do not guess when you are not sure. Use a hint if you are stuck. A hint fills one correct number but costs some bonus points.
