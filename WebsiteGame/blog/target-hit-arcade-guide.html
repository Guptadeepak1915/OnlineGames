Target Hit Precision Guide: Timing & Defeating Bosses

By Deepak Kumar · Published November 2026 · 8 min read

Target Hit is a precision timing arcade challenge. A circular wooden or armored target rotates in the center of the display. Your objective is to launch every allocated blade into the target without striking any previously embedded knife or protruding obstacle. Throwing accurately requires understanding rotational angular velocity and anticipating speed changes.

Here are tested principles to overcome stage rotations, collect bonus apples, and conquer stubborn armored bosses.

1. The Four Rotational Kinematic Patterns

Targets do not simply spin at a constant speed. The physics engine switches dynamically between four specific rotational profiles:

  • Steady Cruise: Smooth, constant clockwise or counter-clockwise rotation. Best for rapid rhythmic tapping.
  • Sinusoidal Acceleration (Pulse): The wheel accelerates to high velocity, then slows to a near standstill before speeding up again. The deceleration trough is your ideal attack window!
  • The Reversal Snap: The wheel spins in one direction, abruptly brakes, and violently reverses direction. Never throw immediately when the wheel slows to zero—wait for the direction to declare itself.
  • Staccato Hitch: The wheel rotates in sudden 45-degree stuttering bursts.

2. Establishing Equidistant Sector Spacing

When you start a stage requiring 8 or 10 knives, do not bunch all your throws into one side of the wheel. Divide the 360-degree rim mentally into quadrants:

Throw your first blade, wait for a half-rotation (180 degrees), and throw your second blade directly opposite. Then bisect those sectors (at 90 degrees and 270 degrees). By distributing knives evenly around the circumference, you ensure that as the remaining target area shrinks, the gaps between blades remain predictable.

3. Slicing Apples for Legendary Blades

Apples pinned to the rim are the primary currency in Target Hit. Hitting an apple slices it in half and awards currency to unlock unique blades (like the Katana, Cyber Blade, and Medieval Dagger) in the weapon locker.

Timing Rule: Throw for an apple on your first or second knife of a stage when the wheel is completely uncluttered. Trying to snipe an apple when 7 sharp knives are already embedded around it drastically increases clash risk.

4. Armored Boss Stages (Every 5th Level)

Every 5 stages, you encounter a Boss Stage (such as the Cheese Wheel, Golden Coin, or Steel Shield). Boss stages feature armored spikes, erratic acceleration profiles, and require 10 to 14 blades to shatter.

Patience is the only way to beat boss stages: do not spam the tap button. Take a full rotational cycle to observe the boss's speed signature before throwing your first blade.
