7 tips to score higher in FlipRun

Published October 2026 · 4 min read

FlipRun looks simple: one tap flips gravity. But the scores of good players are very different from beginners. Here are seven habits that make the difference.

1. Flip early, not late

Your cube needs a short moment to travel from the floor to the ceiling. If you wait until a spike is right in front of you, it is already too late. Flip when the obstacle is still far, and let the cube cross the gap while the obstacle comes closer.

2. Look at the next two obstacles, not one

After level 1 you will see obstacles on both the floor and the ceiling, sometimes close together. Plan your path for two obstacles at once. Ask yourself: after I pass this one, which side do I want to be on?

3. You can flip in mid-air

You do not have to land before flipping again. If you flip too early, you can flip back. This is useful when two obstacles are close together on opposite sides.

4. Coins are worth the risk, mostly

Every coin gives 10 points multiplied by your combo. Collect 3 coins in a row and the multiplier goes up by one, all the way to x5. A single coin at x5 is worth 50 points, which equals a lot of running distance. But do not crash for one coin. A crash ends your run and your combo.

5. Do not miss coins on purpose

If a coin row passes you, your combo drops back to x1. When you are close to a row of coins, try to collect all three. The middle row of coins is the hardest, because you must flip at the right moment and pass through the middle of the screen.

6. Use the "points to beat your best" counter

At the top of the game you can see how far you are from your best score. Treat it as a small target. Beating your best by even 10 points feels good and keeps you playing.

7. Play with sound and a steady rhythm

The flip and landing sounds help you find a rhythm. Try to flip in a steady pattern instead of panicking. If you feel stuck, take a short break. Your reaction time is better when you are relaxed.
