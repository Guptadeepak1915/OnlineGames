6 easy mental maths tricks for adding faster

Published October 2026 · 4 min read

You do not need to be a maths genius to add quickly. These six tricks are simple, and you can practise them while playing puzzles like NumFill.

1. Make tens

Look for numbers that add up to 10 and combine them first.

7 + 8 + 3  →  (7 + 3) + 8 = 10 + 8 = 18

2. Add 9 or 8 by using 10

To add 9, add 10 and take away 1. To add 8, add 10 and take away 2.

46 + 9  →  46 + 10 - 1 = 55
37 + 8  →  37 + 10 - 2 = 45

3. Add from left to right

School teaches adding from the right. In your head, left to right is often easier. Add the tens first, then the ones.

38 + 47  →  30 + 40 = 70,  8 + 7 = 15,  70 + 15 = 85

4. Use doubles

Most people remember doubles well, like 7 + 7 = 14. For nearby numbers, use the double and adjust.

7 + 8  →  7 + 7 + 1 = 15

5. Group numbers

When you add a column of numbers, do not go one by one. Pick pairs that are easy, like 6 + 4 or 5 + 5, and add those groups.

6. Check totals with divisibility rules

These checks are very useful in NumFill, where a total must divide neatly by the clue number.

  • Divisible by 2: the last digit is even.
  • Divisible by 5: the last digit is 0 or 5.
  • Divisible by 3: the digits add up to a multiple of 3. Example: 15 → 1 + 5 = 6, so yes.
  • Divisible by 9: the digits add up to a multiple of 9. Example: 27 → 2 + 7 = 9, so yes.

If your total does not follow the rule for the clue number, you know something is wrong before you submit.

Practise these in NumFill, or take a break with FlipRun.
