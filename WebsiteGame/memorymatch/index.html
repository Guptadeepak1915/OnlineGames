How to play Memory Match

At the start of each level all cards are shown for a short time. Remember where each symbol is. Then the cards turn face down. Tap two cards to flip them. If the symbols are the same, they stay open. If not, they flip back and you lose a life.

Scoring

Each pair gives 10 points multiplied by your combo. Find pairs one after another without a mistake and your combo grows up to x5. A wrong pair resets the combo to x1. When you finish a level you get bonus points for speed, and an extra bonus if you made no mistakes. You also win back one life.

Levels

Level 1 has 4 pairs. The board grows up to 15 pairs. From level 2 onward the cards are shown for a shorter time, so your memory is tested more each level.

Tips

Do not try to remember every card at once. Group the symbols by position, for example the top row, then the second row. Flip cards in a fixed order to learn the board faster. Keep your combo alive by only flipping a second card when you are fairly sure.
