How to play FlipRun

Your cube runs forward by itself. Your only job is to flip gravity at the right time. Tap the screen, click the mouse, or press Space, Up or W. The cube jumps from the floor to the ceiling and back.

Spikes and blocks come from both the floor and the ceiling. Flip early, because the cube needs a moment to cross the gap. Touching any spike or block ends the run.

Scoring and combos

You get points for the distance you run. Coins give bonus points. Collect 3 coins in a row to raise your multiplier, up to x5. If you miss a coin, the multiplier goes back to x1. Every 300 points you reach a new level with new colors, and the game gets faster.

Tips to beat your best score

Do not flip at the last second. Look ahead and plan two obstacles at a time. Coins in the middle of the screen are hard to collect but give the biggest combos. Keep your eyes on the "points to beat your best" counter at the top of the game and push a little further each run.

No login, no download

FlipRun is free and runs in your browser on phone and computer. Your best score is saved on your own device, so it is still there when you come back. If you clear your browser data, the saved score is removed.
