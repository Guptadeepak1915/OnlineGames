About 1Line Wood Craft

1Line Wood Craft is an executive brain-training puzzle inspired by classical Eulerian path mathematics. Featuring a rich, polished mahogany and walnut tactile board with metallic brass inlays, your goal is simple yet challenging: connect all the dots and trace every single edge in one continuous, unbroken stroke without lifting your finger or traversing any line twice.

📐

1. The Golden Rule

Every line on the puzzle board must be carved exactly once. You cannot re-trace an already illuminated golden line.

✨

2. Continuous Stroke

Once you tap or drag from a node, your finger must continue without lifting. If you make a mistake, tap Undo (↩) or Restart (↻).

💡

3. Eulerian Path Hints

If you're stuck, tap the Lightbulb (💡). Euler's theorem proves that a graph can only be solved if it has 0 or exactly 2 odd-degree vertices!
