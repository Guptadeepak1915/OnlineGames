How to play

  1. Goal: put the numbered tiles in order, from 1 upwards, row by row. The empty space ends up in the bottom-right corner.
  2. Move: tap or click a tile next to the empty space and it slides in. Tap a tile in the same row or column as the empty space to slide the whole line.
  3. Keyboard: arrow keys or W A S D slide a tile in that direction. U undo, H hint, P or Esc pause.
  4. Touch: tap a tile, or swipe on the board.
  5. Score: the timer starts on your first move. Fewer moves and a faster time give a better record. Your best time and fewest moves are saved for each difficulty.
  6. Hint and Undo: Hint highlights the next good tile to move. Undo takes back your last move. Both are counted next to your score on the leaderboard.
  7. Daily Challenge: every day has one puzzle, the same for everyone, with its own colours. Finish it on consecutive days to build a daily streak.
  8. Win streak: every puzzle you finish adds one. Leaving a puzzle after you have started it resets the streak.
