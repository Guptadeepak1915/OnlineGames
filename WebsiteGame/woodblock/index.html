How to play Wood Block Puzzle

You get three wooden blocks at a time. Drag a block onto the 8 by 8 board, or tap a block and then tap the board. Blocks cannot be turned, so think about where each shape fits. When every square in a row or column is filled, that line breaks away and leaves free space. When all three blocks are used, you get three new ones. The game ends when none of the blocks you hold can fit on the board.

Scoring

Every square of a block you place gives 1 point. Clearing lines gives 10 points for one line, 40 for two lines at once, 90 for three, and 160 for four. If you clear lines on several moves in a row, your combo multiplies those points, up to x5. Clearing the whole board gives a 300 point bonus.

Tips

Keep the middle of the board open and work from the edges. Do not leave single holes, because they are hard to fill. Look at all three blocks before you place the first one. The glow on the board shows which lines will break if you drop the block there. Blocks that look dim cannot fit anywhere right now, so use the other blocks first.

Wood types

Blocks are made of oak, maple, cherry, walnut, teak and mahogany. The wood only changes the look, so every colour scores the same.
