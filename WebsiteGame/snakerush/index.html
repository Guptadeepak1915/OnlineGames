How to play Snake Rush

Use the arrow keys, WASD or swipe to steer the snake. Eat the red food to grow and score 10 points. Every 5 foods a golden food appears for 5 seconds and gives 50 points. The game ends if you hit the wall or your own body. The snake gets faster every 4 foods.

Tip: plan your path early, keep to the middle when the snake is short, and use the edges only when you have a clear path.
