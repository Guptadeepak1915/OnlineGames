How to Play Snake Rush

Use the arrow keys, WASD or swipe on a touchscreen to steer the snake. Eat the red food dots to grow longer and score 10 points each. The game ends immediately if you steer the snake into the outer wall or into any part of your own body. The snake speeds up every 4 foods, so stay alert as the grid fills up.

Golden Bonus Fruits

Every 5 regular red foods, a glowing golden food appears on the board for exactly 5 seconds. If you can reach it in time, it awards 50 points — five times more than a standard red food. The golden food vanishes after 5 seconds if not collected, so plan your route efficiently but do not take suicidal corners to reach it.

Scoring and Difficulty

Red food: 10 points per piece. Golden food: 50 points per piece. Your best score is saved locally on your device via browser localStorage — no account or login needed. As your score climbs, the snake's speed automatically increases every 4 foods collected, making later stages a true test of reaction time and spatial planning.

Winning Tips

When your snake is short, stay near the center of the board — you have maximum room to maneuver. As your snake grows, switch to a perimeter-hugging S-curve pattern: weave left to right across parallel rows and use the edge lane to return. This "snake-fill" approach maximizes survivability on a crowded board. Never try to cut through a corridor narrower than your snake's current length.
