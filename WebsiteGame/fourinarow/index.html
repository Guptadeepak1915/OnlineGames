How to play Four in a Row

The board has 7 columns and 6 rows. Players take turns dropping a disc into a column. The disc falls to the lowest empty space. The first player to connect four discs in a line, across, up and down, or diagonally, wins the round. If the board is full and nobody has four in a row, the round is a draw.

Run and scoring

Against the computer, your game is a run. A win gives base points: 20 on Easy, 40 on Medium and 80 on Hard. You also get a speed bonus for winning with fewer discs, and a streak bonus of 10 more points for every extra win in a row. A draw gives 10 points and resets your streak. If the computer wins a round, the run ends and your score is saved if it is your best. Who goes first changes every round.

Levels

Easy plays random moves. Medium wins when it can, blocks your winning move and avoids giving you a free win. Hard looks several moves ahead and rarely makes a mistake, so a win on Hard is a real achievement.

Tips to win more

Start in the middle column, because it is part of the most possible lines. Try to make two threats at once, for example three discs in a row that can be finished on both ends. Watch out for diagonals, because they are easy to miss. Before every move, check that your disc does not let your opponent win on the space right above it.
