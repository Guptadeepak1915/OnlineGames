How to play Tic Tac Toe

The board has 9 squares. Players take turns placing their mark. You play X and the computer plays O. The first player to get three marks in a row, across, down or diagonally, wins the round. If all 9 squares are full and nobody has three in a row, the round is a draw.

Run and scoring

Against the computer, your game is a run. A win gives points: 10 on Easy, 20 on Medium and 40 on Hard. Every extra win in a row adds a streak bonus of 5 points more than the last one. A draw gives 5 points and resets your streak, and the run continues. If the computer wins a round, the run ends and your score is saved if it is your best. Who goes first changes every round.

Levels

Easy plays random moves. Medium takes a winning move when it can and blocks yours, but does not plan ahead. Hard plans many moves ahead and rarely makes a mistake, so on Hard a draw is a good result and a win is a real achievement.

Tips to win more

Start in the centre or a corner, because those squares are part of the most winning lines. If the computer takes a corner, take the centre. Always check if your opponent has two in a row and block the third square. Try to create a fork, which is a move that gives you two ways to win at once.
