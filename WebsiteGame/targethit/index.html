How to Play Target Hit

Target Hit tests your reflexes, timing, and pinpoint accuracy! Rotating targets spin at fluctuating speeds and patterns. Your mission is to throw every allocated knife into the target without striking any previously embedded blades.

🎯

1. Tap to Throw

Tap anywhere on screen or press Spacebar to throw a blade vertically into the spinning target rim.

⚡

2. Avoid Previous Blades

Watch out! Striking another knife results in an instant deflection clash and ends your game run.

🍎

3. Slice Bonus Apples

Target apples pinned to the rotating rim for extra points and currency to unlock rare blades in the locker.

👑

4. Armored Boss Stages

Every 5 stages, face armored Boss Targets with erratic speeds, reversing spins, and high stakes!
