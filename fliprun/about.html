About FlipRun

FlipRun is a free one-tap browser game. You flip gravity, dodge spikes and blocks, collect coins and try to beat your own best score.

The game was built by an independent developer from India as a small project. The goal is simple: a fast, fun game that opens instantly on any phone or computer, with no login and no download.

What we care about

  • Games that load fast and work on small phones.
  • No sign up. Your best score is saved on your own device.
  • Fair ads placed outside the game area, so they never block your play.

Have an idea?

New levels, characters and games are planned. If you have feedback or found a bug, please write to us on the Contact page.
